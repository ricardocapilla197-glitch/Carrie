# Detailed Workflow Reference

## CRITICAL: Verified Error Cases (Do NOT Repeat These Mistakes)

### Case 1: Predicative Fronting Misidentified as Normal Subject-Predicate

**Wrong analysis:**
> "By far the largest and tallest of these is Yulong Mountain to the north"
> WRONG: Subject = "By far the largest and tallest of these"

**Correct analysis:**
> CORRECT: This is **predicative fronting**.
> - Normal order: "Yulong Mountain to the north is by far the largest and tallest of these"
> - Inverted: The predicative "By far the largest and tallest of these" is fronted for emphasis
> - Actual subject = "Yulong Mountain to the north"
> - Verb = "is"
> - Verification: Quirk et al. Ch.18.23

**Rule**: When an adjective phrase appears at the beginning of a sentence followed by "be" + noun phrase, ALWAYS check if it is predicative fronting before identifying the subject.

## ABSOLUTE RULE: No Omissions, No Additions

**THIS RULE IS NON-NEGOTIABLE AND OVERRIDES ALL OTHER GUIDANCE.**

When performing sentence-by-sentence analysis (Section 2):

- **EVERY sentence** in the original text MUST be analyzed -- no exceptions
- **NO sentence may be skipped** -- regardless of length, simplicity, or perceived redundancy
- **NO sentence may be fabricated** -- only analyze what exists in the original text
- Sentences must be analyzed in their **exact original order**
- Each sentence must be quoted **verbatim** in the Original sentence field

**Verification step**: After completing Section 2, count the sentences analyzed and confirm the count matches the original text exactly.

Each question with answer letter + detailed explanation in Chinese.

### Section 5: Translation exercises

Fill-in-the-blank table completion with numbered items.

Five subsections covering teaching value, grammar, vocabulary, culture, and reading focus.

### Section 9: Summary writing analysis (概要写作分析)
Three subsections covering discourse pattern identification, 3+2 structure breakdown, and a 60-word model summary.

## Grammar Structure Highlighting Rules

Always bold these structures when verified:

| Structure | Bold Format | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| participial phrase as adverbial | **participial phrase as adverbial** | **present participle phrase**: "Growing unimpressed..." as causal adverbial |
| absolute construction | **absolute construction** | **absolute construction**: "with our screens being our new horizon" |
| subjunctive mood | **subjunctive mood** | **subjunctive mood**: "couldn't have posed" contrary to past fact |
| inversion | **inversion** | **inversion**: "weird as that is" concessive clause inversion |
| extraposition | **extraposition** | **extraposition**: "It" is formal subject, real subject is that-clause |
| cleft sentence | **cleft sentence** | **cleft sentence**: "did succeed" uses did for emphasis |
| appositive clause | **appositive clause** | **appositive clause**: "that..." explains content of "assumption" |
| parallel structure | **parallel structure** | **parallel structure**: "to summarize... to generate... and to write" |
| parenthetical | **parenthetical** | **parenthetical**: "it turned out" means "results showed" |
| gerund as subject | **gerund as subject** | **gerund as subject**: "Using calculators..." as sentence subject |
| infinitive as subject | **infinitive as subject** | **infinitive as subject**: "To find paper..." as subject |
| past participle phrase | **past participle phrase** | **past participle phrase**: "published in..." modifies "A study" |
| present participle phrase | **present participle phrase** | **present participle phrase**: "delivering..." as result adverbial |
| appositive | **appositive** | **appositive**: "a graduate student..." supplements identity |
| transferred negation | **transferred negation** | **transferred negation**: "I don't think..." negation transfers to subordinate clause |

## Common Article Types and Focus Areas

### Argumentative
- Focus on: thesis statement, arguments, evidence, counterarguments, conclusion
- Highlight: author's stance, rhetorical devices, logical connectors

### Expository
- Focus on: topic sentence, supporting details, cause-effect relationships
- Highlight: definition sentences, classification, process description

### News Report
- Focus on: lead paragraph, inverted pyramid structure, attribution
- Highlight: quoted speech, passive voice for objectivity, factual statements

### Research Report
- Focus on: research question, methodology, findings, implications
- Highlight: hedging language, statistical references, academic vocabulary

### Narrative
- Focus on: chronological order, character development, descriptive language
- Highlight: direct/indirect speech, sensory details, figurative language

## Summary Writing Analysis Template (概要写作分析)

This section (Section 9) provides a complete summary writing guide for the article, following the Shanghai Senior High School English Summary Writing framework.

### 9.1 Discourse Pattern Identification (语篇模式判定)

Identify which of the five common discourse patterns the article follows:

#### Pattern 1: General-Specific (总分结构)
- **Structure**: General statement + supporting details/examples
- **Key markers**: topic sentence at beginning, followed by elaboration
- **Necessary components**: (1) General claim/thesis, (2) Supporting evidence/examples
- **Typical in**: argumentative essays, expository texts

#### Pattern 2: Question-Answer (提问与回答结构)
- **Structure**: Question posed + answer(s) provided + explanation
- **Key markers**: rhetorical question at beginning, followed by response
- **Necessary components**: (1) Central question, (2) Answer/response, (3) Supporting explanation
- **Typical in**: exploratory essays, scientific inquiries

#### Pattern 3: Claim-Counterclaim (主张与反主张结构)
- **Structure**: Claim presented + counterclaim introduced + resolution
- **Key markers**: however, yet, but signaling the counterclaim
- **Necessary components**: (1) Initial claim, (2) Counterclaim/challenge, (3) Evidence/position resolution
- **Typical in**: debates, opinion pieces

#### Pattern 4: Problem-Solution (问题解决结构)
- **Structure**: Problem identified + solution(s) proposed + evaluation
- **Key markers**: problem vocabulary (issue, challenge, difficulty), solution signals
- **Necessary components**: (1) Problem statement, (2) Proposed solution(s), (3) Result/evaluation
- **Typical in**: research reports, policy papers

#### Pattern 5: Cause-Effect (因果结构)
- **Structure**: Cause(s) identified + effect(s) described + implications
- **Key markers**: because, lead to, result in, therefore, thus
- **Necessary components**: (1) Cause(s), (2) Effect(s), (3) Implications/conclusions
- **Typical in**: analytical essays, scientific reports

**Analysis format**:
```markdown
## 9. 概要写作分析

### 语篇模式判定

本文属于 **[Pattern Name]（[Chinese name]）** 结构。

**判定依据：** [2-3 sentences explaining why this pattern was chosen, citing specific textual evidence]

**必要组成部分：**
1. [Component 1]: [content from the article]
2. [Component 2]: [content from the article]
3. [Component 3 if applicable]: [content from the article]
```

### 9.2 3+2 Structure Breakdown (3+2结构划分)

The "3+2" structure is the core framework for summary writing:

- **"3" = Three core main points** extracted from the article's discourse pattern:
  - For General-Specific: thesis + 2 key supporting points
  - For Question-Answer: question + answer + explanation
  - For Claim-Counterclaim: claim + counterclaim + resolution
  - For Problem-Solution: problem + solution + result
  - For Cause-Effect: cause + effect + implication

- **"2" = Two expression techniques**:
  - Technique 1: Proper use of **connectors** (showing logical relationships between points)
  - Technique 2: Effective **paraphrasing** (using own words, avoiding direct copying)

**Structure breakdown format**:
```markdown
### 3+2结构划分

**3个核心要点：**

要点1（[type, e.g. 总起/论点/问题]）：
[1-2 sentence summary of the first main point in Chinese]
→ 英文表达：[English paraphrase]

要点2（[type, e.g. 论据/回答/解决方案]）：
[1-2 sentence summary of the second main point in Chinese]
→ 英文表达：[English paraphrase]

要点3（[type, e.g. 结论/结果/影响]）：
[1-2 sentence summary of the third main point in Chinese]
→ 英文表达：[English paraphrase]

**2个表达技巧：**

技巧1——逻辑连接：
[说明如何使用连接词将三个要点串联，列举可用的连接词]
→ 例如：[show example connectors]

技巧2——同义替换：
[列出原文关键词及其替换表达，避免直接复制]
→ 原文：[original word] → 替换：[replacement 1], [replacement 2]
→ 原文：[original word] → 替换：[replacement 1], [replacement 2]
```

### 9.3 60-Word Model Summary (60词概要范文)

**Requirements**:
- Exactly 60 words (target: 55-65 words range)
- Must cover all 3 main points from the 3+2 structure
- Must use own words (paraphrasing, not direct copying)
- Must show logical flow between points
- Must be a standalone paragraph
- Grading: Content 5 points + Language 5 points = 10 points total

**Writing formula by pattern**:

| Pattern | Formula (3-point structure) |
|---------|----------------------------|
| General-Specific | Thesis (20 words) + Evidence (20 words) + Significance (20 words) |
| Question-Answer | Question (15 words) + Answer (25 words) + Explanation (20 words) |
| Claim-Counterclaim | Claim (20 words) + Counter-argument (20 words) + Resolution (20 words) |
| Problem-Solution | Problem (15 words) + Solution (25 words) + Result (20 words) |
| Cause-Effect | Cause (20 words) + Effect (20 words) + Implication (20 words) |

**Model summary format**:
```markdown
### 60词概要范文

[English summary text, exactly ~60 words]

（词数：[count] words）

**内容核对：**
- 要点1是否覆盖？✓ / ✗
- 要点2是否覆盖？✓ / ✗
- 要点3是否覆盖？✓ / ✗

**语言特点分析：**
- 连接手段：[list the connectors used]
- 同义替换：[list paraphrased expressions]
- 句式特点：[note any complex sentence structures used]
- 语法亮点：[highlight any notable grammar features]
```
