## 1. 强调句 ★★★

结构：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分。

- *It is when residents create a piece of art together that a true sense of belonging emerges.*
- *It is the shared experience, not the artwork itself, that binds a community.*

## 2. 倒装句 ★★★

常用触发词：Not only... but (also) / Only when/if / Never / Rarely / So + adj. + that / 省略 if 的虚拟倒装。

- *Not only did the project break the ice among strangers, but it also gave everyone a reason to return.*
- *Only when people work toward a common goal do they develop genuine trust.*
- *Were a city to remove these shared landmarks, the community would lose a visible reminder of its unity.*（兼虚拟语气）

## 3. 非谓语结构

doing / done / to do 作状语、定语或宾补，压缩句子、增加层次。

- *Neighbors spent one summer building a sculpture, sharing stories that inspired its design.*
- *Faced with budget cuts, the school turned to volunteers for help.*
- *The festival aims to bring local artists into public view.*

## 4. 定语从句

限定性与非限定性搭配使用；注意 which/who/whose/where 的准确选择。

- *The mural, which was painted by local students, has become a landmark.*
- *Students who participate in group projects learn to negotiate different perspectives.*

## 5. 状语从句

原因（because/since）、让步（although/while）、条件（if/unless）、时间（when/as）等，体现逻辑关系。

- *Although the plan requires funding, it costs far less than building a new facility.*
- *Identity grows from shared experiences, because people bond over what they do together.*

## 6. 名词性从句（同位语从句 ★★★）

主语/宾语/表语/同位语从句。同位语从句（the fact/idea/belief that...）尤其加分。

- *What makes such art irreplaceable is the fact that every resident can point to it and say, "I helped build that."*
- *I share Kelly's view that public art plays a pivotal role in shaping community identity.*
- *There is no doubt that effort deserves recognition.*

## 7. 虚拟语气 ★★★

与现在/过去事实相反的假设、建议类动词后的 should 型、were 倒装式。

- *If attendance were higher, the club could invite more renowned filmmakers.*
- *I would suggest that the committee reserve part of the budget for promotion.*
- *Without such programs, many talented students would never discover their interests.*

## 8. it 作形式宾语

主语 + find/consider/make/think + it + 宾补 + to do / that 从句。

- *I find it hard to imagine any social program achieving the same effect.*
- *Smartphones make it possible for students to access lectures anywhere.*

## 9. with 复合结构 / 独立主格 ★★★

with + 名词 + doing/done/adj./介词短语，或独立主格作伴随状语，瞬间提升画面感。

- *Neighbors spent one summer building a sculpture in the central square, with children collecting stones and elderly residents sharing stories.*
- *The deadline approaching, the team worked late into the night.*（独立主格）

## 10. 其他常用固定句型

地道搭配与惯用表达（非模板套话），示例如：

- *a sense of belonging / break the ice / play a pivotal role in / point to / be conducive to*
- *There is no denying that... / It goes without saying that...*（适量，避免堆砌）
- *be likely to do / serve as / contribute to / be worth doing*

---

## 范文使用原则

1. **一篇 130–180 词的范文通常可自然容纳 8–10 种句型**；不必强求十种全上，自然优先。
2. 句型分布在论述展开处，**不要堆在开头结尾**——官方明确反感仪式化的背诵框架。
3. 范文后逐条标注：句型名称 + 引用的原文短语。
